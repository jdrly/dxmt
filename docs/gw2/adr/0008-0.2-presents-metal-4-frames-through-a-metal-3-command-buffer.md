# 0.2 presents Metal 4 frames through a Metal 3 command buffer, and 0.1.x keeps the Metal 4 present

Decided 2026-11-13. Status: accepted.

On the arm64 engine, moving the cursor on Metal 4 made the frame interval saw between 16.7 and 33
ms, about 40 fps, even on the character screen (#223). The cause is how macOS hands a Metal 4
present's drawable back. A Metal 3 present is released by the surface's use count (`inuse` 1). A
Metal 4 present is released by a window server signal (`inuse` 0), and that signal comes a refresh
late two frames in five while the pointer moves. I played five Metal 4 present orders and all of
them sawed. Presenting through a Metal 3 command buffer (`DXMT_MTL4_PRESENT=mtl3`, #227) is flat, so
that is how 0.2 presents. The app sets it (app PR #153). The fork's own default stays `mtl4`,
because 0.1.x sets neither switch, runs the native Metal 4 present, and is flat on it.

## Consequences

- With `mtl3` a queue on Metal 4 makes two shared events, `frame` and `shown`. The present pass goes
  into a buffer of the queue's Metal 3 command queue, which is the chunk's last record.
- On my Lion's Arch laps Metal 4 with `mtl3` ran about 58 fps median against about 50.5 on Metal 3.
  That is one Metal 3 run, not an alternated comparison.
- A Metal 3 present gets its drawable back a refresh later than a Metal 4 one (`aftergpu` about 32
  ms against 48). With the first placement of the `shown` wait that cost 0.2 a refresh of headroom
  in combat. ADR 0009 is the answer to that.
- The two other test switches of #223 lost and are deleted: `DXMT_MTL4_PRESENT=done` (#242) and
  `DXMT_MTL4_LAYER_SET` (#226), by #277 under ADR 0010.
- The README's row called `DXMT_MTL4_PRESENT` a test switch for #223 that stays off, which was
  wrong once the app shipped `mtl3` on 0.2. The fork's own default stays `mtl4`, because flipping it
  would change 0.1.x, which sets neither switch. 0.1.3 and 0.1.4 both ship that way. The audit's
  plan 018, a status column for the switch table, corrected the row on 2026-11-17 (#320, PR #321):
  `mtl4` is the fork's default and what 0.1.x runs, and the app sets `mtl3` on 0.2.

## Alternatives I rejected

- **Staying on Metal 3 on 0.2.** That was the interim state on 2026-11-12 and 13. It is flat, but
  slower on the same laps, and its encode thread runs longer (1.86 against 1.58 ms a frame).
- **Another Metal 4 present order.** Five saw the same, among them a present after the pass's commit
  completed (`done`) and `DXMT_DISPLAY_SYNC=0`.
- **A layer over the game that makes the window server composite.** It cured the stutter in a test
  with a floating window, and I turned it down as a band-aid. 0.1.3 runs right on the same Mac, so
  the difference between the stacks was the bug to find.
- **Deleting the native Metal 4 present.** The audit of 2026-11-16 proposed it (ARCH-08), and its
  verifier corrected it: 0.1.x ships the native present and holds 60 on it.
